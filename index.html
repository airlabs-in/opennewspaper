CYCLONE ARNAB APPROACHES ANDHRA COAST; EVACUATIONS BEGIN

Andhra Pradesh moves people from vulnerable areas as Odisha mobilises for an October 28 landfall

ANDHRA Pradesh has begun evacuations from vulnerable areas as Cyclone Arnab strengthens over the Bay of Bengal, with landfall forecast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam on October 28.

Andhra officials have ordered evacuations from low-lying areas and moved 4,711 pregnant women to health centres and hospitals. Schools in the listed districts are to close from October 27 to 31.

The India Meteorological Department forecasts sustained winds of 115–125 km/h, with gusts up to 140 km/h. Odisha has also mobilised ministers and restricted fishing in vulnerable coastal districts.

Disaster-response teams are on standby as authorities issue public alerts and move residents to safer locations. ...more
